🎯 Skeet VR

For a phone in a Google Cardboard viewer. Clay targets fly across in front of you; shoot them with a finger gun before they land.

Aim

How it aims
Camera lens

Fisheye sees more of the room and keeps your hand in view further out to the sides, but your hand looks smaller to the hand finder. Regular sees less, with your hand larger and steadier. Auto uses the fisheye unless it has failed twice on this phone.

How to play

1. Press Start and allow motion (and the camera, if asked). 2. Turn the phone sideways and put it in the viewer. 3. Fire once to begin (drop your thumb, or press the viewer's button). 4. A round is 20 clays, some thrown two at a time. A beep comes just before each throw. The viewer's button always fires too. Turn the phone upright to come back here.

Open on your phone

Scan this with the phone's camera. It opens this page.

Your rounds

Rounds are kept in this browser only. The camera picture is used on this phone to find your hand and is never stored or sent anywhere. Each round records clays hit, shots fired, how the thumb trigger read, and how often the hand was lost, so the trigger can be tuned.
